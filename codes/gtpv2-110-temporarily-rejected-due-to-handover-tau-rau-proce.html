GTPv2 cause 110 Temporarily rejected due to handover/TAU/RAU procedure in progress

GTPv2-C cause3GPP TS 29.274 §8.4 (Table 8.4-1)

What it means

The request was temporarily rejected because a handover/TAU/RAU procedure was already in progress for this subscriber -- usually resolves on its own once that procedure finishes.

Where you see it

In the Cause IE of a GTPv2-C response (Create Session, Modify Bearer, Delete Session…) on S11, S5 or S8.

What to check

Check which node sent the response (MME, SGW or PGW): the cause belongs to that node's decision.
