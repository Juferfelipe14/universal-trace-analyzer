EMM cause #18 CS domain not available

EMM cause (LTE NAS)3GPP TS 24.301 §9.9.3.9 and Annex A

What it means

CS domain not available: circuit-switched services (CSFB/SMS over SGs) aren't available; LTE data works.

Where you see it

In LTE NAS Attach reject, Tracking area update reject, Service reject or Detach request.

What to check

Check the SGs/MSC configuration if CS fallback or SMS is expected.

Look at the S6a (MME↔HSS) and S11 messages just before the reject: they usually show the real trigger.
