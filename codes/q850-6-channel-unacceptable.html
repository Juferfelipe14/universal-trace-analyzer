Q.850 cause 6 Channel unacceptable

Q.850 causeITU-T Q.850 Table 1

What it means

The channel selected for the call wasn't acceptable to the far end (Channel unacceptable).

Where you see it

In ISUP/BICC REL messages, 2G/3G DTAP Disconnect, and SIP Reason headers (Q.850;cause=…).

What to check

Check the cause location (user, private or public network) to see which side released the call.
