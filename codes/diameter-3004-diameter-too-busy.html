Diameter 3004 DIAMETER_TOO_BUSY

DiameterRFC 6733 §7.1 · 3GPP TS 29.272 §7.4.3 · RFC 4006

What it means

The peer is too busy to handle the request right now (DIAMETER_TOO_BUSY) -- a load problem on that node, not a rejection of the subscriber.

Where you see it

In the Result-Code or Experimental-Result-Code AVP of a Diameter answer (S6a, S6d, Gx, Gy, Cx…).

What to check

Ask the operator of the node that answered to check its load; the request can be retried to an alternate peer.

Look at the Origin-Host of the answer: an intermediate agent (DRA/IPX) and the home HSS mean different owners of the problem.
