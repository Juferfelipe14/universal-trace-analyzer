GTPv1 cause 207 GPRS connection suspended

GTPv1-C cause3GPP TS 29.060 §7.7.1

What it means

The subscriber's GPRS connection is currently suspended (e.g. during a 2G/3G voice call).

Where you see it

In the Cause IE of a GTPv1-C response (Create PDP Context, Update PDP Context…) on Gn/Gp.

What to check

Check which GSN sent the response: the cause belongs to that node's decision.
